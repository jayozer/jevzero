## Interface concept

Asset: `jevzero-concept.png`

```
Use case: ui-mockup. Design a polished, production-quality desktop SaaS dashboard for "JevZero", an intelligent Gmail triage app. This is a deliberate reinvention: art-directed, editorial and quietly confident, not a generic purple AI dashboard. 1600x1100 landscape, crisp interface screenshot, no device frame. Left sidebar 220px wide deep almost-black forest green, tiny warm lime geometric zero mark and cream "JevZero" wordmark. Nav: Overview, Inbox, Needs attention, Review queue, Rules, Activity. Main canvas warm ivory #f6f5ef. Header breadcrumb Workspace / Overview, search box, small DEMO badge and circular avatar. Greeting headline large dark forest grotesk: "Clear inbox. Clear head." Subtitle "A little intelligence. A lot less noise." Right header action forest button "Connect Gmail". Below is a wide pale-sage feature panel: left small eyebrow YOUR DAILY BRIEF, big "12 emails. 4 need you.", subtext "Start with what matters. Let the rest wait.", compact button "Review your inbox". Right half contains an elegant sculptural translucent sage-glass zero-loop and folded paper envelope on a pale sage background, subtle grain and soft shadow. Below feature panel 3 understated inline metrics separated by thin rules: 4 Needs attention, 2 To review, 5 Can wait. Lower center-left a refined white email list with tab "All mail", "Reply needed", "Newsletters", filter pills, sender monogram circles, fine hairline dividers, sender Maya Chen, subject "Launch review — your sign-off is the last step", muted preview, small olive "Work" and terracotta "Reply needed" tags. Lower right selected email detail pane: sender and subject, short body, outlined card "Jev's take", category "Work & projects", confidence 96%, subtle confidence bar, label preview and forest green button "Approve labels". Lots of disciplined negative space; functional dense list, clean typography 14-16px body, no tiny illegible text, no charts for decoration, no gradients over the whole page, no emoji. Sophisticated muted sage, ivory, forest and terracotta palette, restrained 12px corner radii. Render the complete GUI composition with realistic readable text.
```
